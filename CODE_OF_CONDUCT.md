# CollaborationOS Community Code of Conduct

We want CollaborationOS collaboration to be rigorous, respectful, and safe.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss evidence and decisions without attacking people;
- identify uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and source limitations;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, copyright, and host-project boundaries;
- accept good-faith challenge and correction;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, or unwanted
  disclosure of personal information;
- avoid using AI-generated volume to overwhelm review or fabricate consensus.

Unacceptable behavior may be removed from project spaces. Repeated or severe
violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion by the maintainers.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security or
contact channel listed on the GitHub repository. Do not expose sensitive details
in a public issue. Maintainers should acknowledge reports, limit disclosure,
avoid conflicts of interest, and document the resolution proportionately.

This policy governs project community spaces. It does not replace applicable
law, employer policy, or platform terms.
